Privacy Policy

Stateside Smiles

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Stateside Smiles ("we", "our", or "the Service") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data we process. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Stateside Smiles, we may collect the following information:

Data Type Source Purpose
Account email and name Registration Authentication, notifications
YouTube channel info YouTube OAuth Video publishing, analytics display
Facebook page info Facebook OAuth Video publishing to your Page
TikTok account info TikTok OAuth Video publishing, account display
Generated video files AI pipeline Content creation and publishing

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Authenticate you and manage your account
  • Publish AI-generated videos to your connected social media accounts (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok)
  • Display account statistics and publishing status in the dashboard
  • Send email or Slack notifications about pipeline activity
  • Improve the quality of generated content

4. TikTok Data Usage

When you connect your TikTok account via OAuth, we request the following permissions:

  • video.publish — To upload and publish videos to your TikTok profile
  • user.info.basic — To display your TikTok username and profile information in the dashboard

We access your TikTok data solely for the purpose of publishing videos you have authorized through the Stateside Smiles pipeline. We do not access your TikTok messages, contacts, or private content. We do not sell or share your TikTok data with any third parties.

You can revoke TikTok access at any time by visiting TikTok Security Settings and removing Stateside Smiles from your authorized apps.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers with the following protections:

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and never exposed in logs or API responses
  • Database connections use TLS encryption
  • Generated video files are stored temporarily and can be deleted on request
  • Access to the admin dashboard requires authentication

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • With social media platforms (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok) as necessary to publish content you have authorized
  • With AI service providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Replicate, ElevenLabs) as necessary to generate content — no personal data is shared with these providers
  • As required by law or legal process

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Generated video files are retained for up to 90 days after creation. OAuth tokens are retained until you disconnect your account or they expire. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Revoke access — Disconnect any connected social media account at any time
  • Data portability — Request your data in a machine-readable format

9. Cookies

The Service uses minimal cookies for authentication (JWT session tokens). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
